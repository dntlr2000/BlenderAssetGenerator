# V0.6 guarded revision prompt

Convert supported QA findings into the smallest ID/path-addressed suggestions. Preserve all unrelated subtrees. A generated target alone may produce only a manual-review suggestion. Do not modify external custom-mesh payloads automatically. Compile and apply nothing until the user explicitly approves exact candidate IDs. Do not encode material identity additions or object material reassignment as an ordinary geometry revision; route an exact shared-identity `scope_change` to Material Identity Split and stop at its specialized approval request.
